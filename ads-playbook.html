4 Upload Images & Logo ▼

Use the same images and logo as Campaign A. We only want to test the ad COPY, not the visuals.

Upload the SMC gear logo as the square logo.

Use the same stock images or suggested images for landscape/square/portrait.

Add the same search themes you used in Campaign A (Google may suggest them automatically).

5 Publish & Set Budget Alert ▼

Review everything, then hit Publish. Leave it RUNNING.

Then set up a budget rule:

Go to Tools > Rules > New Rule:

  • Apply to: This campaign
  • Action: Pause campaign
  • Condition: Cost > $20 (one week of spend)
  • Frequency: Daily
  • Email notification: ON

Do the same rule for Campaign A if you haven't already.

6 Lower Campaign A Budget ▼

Go to SMC - Quote Seekers (Campaign A) and change the daily budget from $5.00 to $2.50.

Optionally rename it to SMC - Quote Seekers A to keep the A/B naming clear.

Both campaigns should now be running at $2.50/day = $5/day total.

Week 1-2 Optimization Playbook

After the A/B test period, we'll make data-driven decisions.

A Review Search Terms Report ▼

We'll pull the actual search terms people typed to trigger your ads via MCP.

Kill any irrelevant terms (add as negative keywords in the dashboard).

Double down on terms that got clicks/conversions.

B Pick the Winner ▼

Compare Campaign A vs B on:

  • CTR (click-through rate) - which ad gets more clicks per impression
  • Cost per click - which one is cheaper
  • Conversions - which one actually gets form submissions
  • Cost per conversion - the ultimate metric

Target: Under $20 per lead for quote requests.

Kill the loser. Give the winner the full $5/day budget.

C Iterate on the Winner ▼

Once we have a winner, create a new B variant to test against it.

Possible next tests: urgency copy, different CTAs, location-specific targeting, different landing pages.

We'll update this playbook with the winning copy each round.
